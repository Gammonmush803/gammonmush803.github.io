## 🎯 What Does This App Do?

Imagine writing a message or document, but instead of typing each word, you just speak. Here's exactly how it works:

1. **Press and hold** your left mouse button on any text input box

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting

### Microphone Not Working

- Check if your microphone is plugged in and selected as the default device
- Look at your system's privacy settings to ensure microphone access is enabled
- Try testing with your system's voice recorder to confirm functionality

### Text Not Appearing

- Ensure you're holding the mouse button down on the actual input field
- Wait a moment for the recognition to start - the floating window should appear
- Try restarting the application

### Slow Performance

- Close other resource-heavy applications during use
- Reduce the size of other windows if the floating display is obstructed
- Test with simpler words to verify functionality
